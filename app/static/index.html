Fire the same kiln with less energy and fewer cracked pots.

How it works

  1. 1. Describe the jobClay body, load weight, number of pieces, moisture, room temperature and the defect rate you can accept.
  2. 2. The agent optimizesIt searches thousands of schedules and keeps the lowest-energy one that meets your defect target.
  3. 3. Safety check, then you approveA Cedar policy checks every limit. Only an approved schedule can be exported.
